PROJECT

Dimensional Ruler

You are sphere being that can move from one dimension to another but you need other sphere beings help to do so, your goal is to keep cloase as many sphere beings as you can. When a sphere being leaves the screen your power get decrease and you start to go to lower dimensions. You can prevent sphere beings from leaving by switching place with them. The more you prevent the sphere beings from leaving the more your score increases on the other hand if more than 10 sphere beings leave the screen your game gets over.

Ray Tracer

A pygame project in which you can play with light rays , mirror and refracting materials . You can add as many of these as you like , change their sizes and rotate them too. Basically you have full control over how to set up your enviroment
